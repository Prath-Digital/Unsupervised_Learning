# Scenario: New Smartphone Customer Segmentation

A new smartphone has been launched in the market. The company has collected basic information about 1,000 customers, including their age and annual income.

The company does not have predefined customer categories. It wants to discover natural groups of customers using unsupervised machine learning.

Your task is to use the **K-Means Clustering algorithm** to:

1. Analyze the customer data.
2. Find suitable customer clusters.
3. Group customers based on Age and Annual Income.
4. Visualize the clusters.
5. Identify the characteristics of each cluster.
6. Help the smartphone company understand which customer segments it can target with different marketing strategies.
