# Customer Segmentation for an Online Retail Store

An online retail store wants to understand its customers better and create targeted marketing campaigns.

The store has collected information about 1,000 customers, including their annual income and spending score. However, the company does not have predefined customer categories.

Your task is to use Hierarchical Clustering, an unsupervised machine learning algorithm, to discover natural groups of customers based on their income and spending behavior.

The company wants to identify groups such as customers with high income and high spending, high income and low spending, low income and high spending, and other naturally occurring customer segments.
